# Claimed Seats skip the purchase-country check

A discounted Individual Purchase only works in the country it was bought from (ADR-0009). The country checked is the buyer's newest Individual Purchase of the course with no Approved Refund Request. A Student whose Enrollment came from a Claimed Seat has no Purchase of their own, so nothing is checked, even though Team Licences are sold at purchasing-power prices too. We keep it that way for now: a Team Admin buys for people who may live anywhere, and blocking a Seat on the Team Licence's country would lock out exactly the Students the Team Admin bought for.

## Consequences

A Team Admin in a discounted country can buy Seats cheaply for Students in a full-price country.
