# Cadence

An online course platform where Buyers purchase courses and Students take them.

## Language

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A Buyer's ask to get their money back for a purchase, which is Requested until an Admin Approves or Declines it. A Declined Refund Request is final: that purchase can't be asked about again.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback, Refund (for a request not yet Approved)

**Refund**:
The full amount paid for a purchase, returned to its Buyer when a Refund Request is Approved, which also ends what the purchase gave: the Buyer's Enrollment for an Own Purchase, every Seat for a Team Purchase. A purchase is refunded whole or not at all.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation

**Refund Eligibility**:
The single set of conditions a purchase must meet for its Buyer to raise a Refund Request. An Own Purchase is judged by how much of the course its Buyer has taken; a Team Purchase qualifies only while none of its Seats is claimed, and no Seat can be claimed while it has a Refund Request open.
_Avoid_: Cancellation offer, cancel window

### Purchases

**Purchase**:
A Buyer's payment for one course, either an Own Purchase or a Team Purchase.
_Avoid_: Order, transaction

**Own Purchase**:
A Buyer's purchase of a course for themselves, which covers no Seats.
_Avoid_: Personal purchase, individual purchase

**Team Purchase**:
A single purchase by a Buyer that covers several Seats for one course.

**Seat**:
One person's place on a course from a Team Purchase, either unclaimed or claimed; claiming a Seat gives that person an Enrollment. The Seats of a refunded Team Purchase can never be claimed.
_Avoid_: Coupon, licence, Redeem (for claim)

**Enrollment**:
A Student's access to one course, gained through an Own Purchase or a claimed Seat.
_Avoid_: Access (as a noun), registration

### People

**Buyer**:
The person who paid for a purchase, who may never take the course themselves.
_Avoid_: Customer, purchaser

**Admin**:
A member of Cadence staff who runs the platform, including deciding Refund Requests.
_Avoid_: Team admin (for the Buyer of a Team Purchase), moderator, reviewer

**Student**:
A person who takes courses on the platform.
_Avoid_: Learner

**Instructor**:
A person who creates and publishes courses on Cadence.
_Avoid_: Teacher, author

### Comments

**Comment**:
A post someone makes on a lesson. Staff for the course and Students who can view its content may comment.
_Avoid_: Post, Message

**Reply**:
A Comment made on another Comment. Replies go one level deep: you can't reply to a Reply.

**Question**:
A top-level Comment by someone who isn't staff for that course (its Instructor or an Admin).

**Answered**:
A Question that has at least one Reply from staff for the course.
_Avoid_: Resolved, Closed

**Thread Participant**:
The author of a top-level Comment, or anyone who has replied to it.

### Notifications

**Notification**:
A message to one person telling them that something which concerns them has happened, pointing them to where they can see or act on it. Each Notification has exactly one recipient.
_Avoid_: Alert, Event (an event is the thing that happened, not the message about it)

**Unread**:
A Notification its recipient hasn't opened or marked read yet. The bell counts Unread Notifications, and a recipient can mark one Unread again.
_Avoid_: Unseen, New
